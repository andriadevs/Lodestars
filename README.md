## About:
This mod adds a single item, the Lodestar, which can be crafted with one nether star, two netherite ingots, one eye of ender, and one compass.

While holding it, you can crouch and right-click a lodestone to attune yourself to it. Using the item will then teleport you to your attuned lodestone. You can only attune yourself to a single lodestone! There is no penalty to changing your attuned lodestone, however, so feel free to change your attuned lodestone as much as you want.

![Lodestar crafting recipe, requires a nether star in the center slot, a compass below, netherite ingots to the left and right, and an eye of ender above.](https://github.com/andriadevs/Lodestars/blob/main/recipe.png?raw=true)

This mod also has a few configs to modify how the lodestar functions! By default, players will attune themselves to lodestones, but you can also have lodestars individually attune themselves to lodestones. There are options for enabling/disabling durability, tweaking durability costs per use, and more!

_Feel free to use this mod in modpacks!_
